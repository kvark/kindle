## Mechanism and numerical contract

The [diagnosis](2026-10-12-jax-gap-accounting.md) found an RGB recurrent
projection launching only eight workgroups. Long BF16 dense products now
partition K when their 32x32 output grid is underfilled: K≥1024, target128
workgroups, at least256 reduction elements per partition, cap16 partitions.
Existing F32 SumRows combines partials before output rounding. No atomics or
new reduction shader. Short/occupied products and fused addends stay unsplit;
convolution, grouped products and storage precision are unchanged.

The extra reductions/casts increase RGB graph dispatches6,562→6,827 and CDP
6,908→6,980; execution improves despite this. Sampled Vulkan budget headroom
stays above13.69 GiB RGB /10.51 GiB CDP. These are estimated budget samples,
not physical-free or peak-memory claims. Four production world submissions stay.

Independent checks pass:32 full-product/partial cases, **859,216 exact F64
comparisons**, ragged dimensions, transposed A/B, rounded outputs, poisoned and
reused buffers;12 long-product master/reduced-gradient cases. Unchanged18
forward/master,18 reduced-gradient,32 connected Adam updates and16 convolution
cases also pass. CPU selection, actual uniform binding, cache and SPIR-V tests
pass; no numerical tolerance was relaxed.

Both pairs start with exact292/346 tensors. All272 reports, first targets and
initial/first/final tensors are finite. Controls exactly reproduce the retained
split-convolution fixtures. **Candidate stochastic trajectories are not exact.**
RGB's first recurrent deterministic state differs by0.052% relative L2; CDP's
differs by2.59%, with categorical samples also changing. Final combined saved
tensors, including optimizer state, differ by8.87%/84.85% relative L2. Finite
synthetic updates do not establish retained game quality; keep that as a later
learning gate. Raw differences are retained, not hidden behind a parity claim.
