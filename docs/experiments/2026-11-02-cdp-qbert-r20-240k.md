## Question and evidence

Can the current Qbert recipe trade some replay for more experience within the
30-minute training budget? The retained R24 learners spend 81.49% and 81.23% of
their training time in learning updates. Their greedy scores are 16,525.000 and
14,913.542, each with 24/24 first pyramids. The original 15k gate passes on one
of two learners. Crossed evaluation changes the weaker learner's near-threshold
outcome but leaves the stronger learner ahead on both cohorts. The failed
original confirmation remains failed.

The proposed full comparison is fresh 240k/R20 against retained 200k/R24 on
seed16001: 20% more experience, with 18,721 versus 18,715 scheduled updates.
This changes experience, cadence and replay sampling together. It is a budget
trade, not an isolated replay-ratio effect or a claim that more experience helps.
The current check changes only R24 to R20 and runs 8,192 fresh actions.

Earlier larger changes regressed: [300k at RGB48/H5/R24](../results/2026-11-01-cdp-qbert-300k.md)
lowered greedy14,339.583 to13,861.458 and sampled13,761.458 to8,668.750, while
adding both experience and updates. The older RGB48/H15 400k/R8 comparison
lowered greedy11,984.375 to10,042.708 and cost40m35s versus30m27s. Neither result
is erased. This smaller trade uses the subsequent RGB64/H5/dynamics1e-4/
replay-value1 recipe; no old life is extended and no prior experience is loaded.

An approximate full-cost model scales learning time by18721/18715 and the
remaining elapsed time by1.2. Applied to the two retained lives it predicts
1,594.246s/1,541.909s, or1,753.670s/1,696.100s with10% margin. These are estimates,
not measured performance. The fixed bounded projection below decides feasibility.
